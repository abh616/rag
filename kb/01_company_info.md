# IndoGulf Trading Pvt. Ltd. — Company Information

## Who We Are
IndoGulf Trading Pvt. Ltd. is a Hyderabad-based import/export trading company established in 2011. We specialize in agricultural commodities, spices, and industrial raw materials. We connect Indian suppliers with buyers across the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Africa.

## Our Presence
- **Headquarters:** Hyderabad, Telangana, India
- **Branch Office:** Dubai, UAE (liaison office)
- **Years in Business:** 13+ years
- **Annual Trade Volume:** 2,000+ metric tons

## What We Do
- Source quality products directly from Indian farmers and manufacturers
- Handle end-to-end export documentation
- Arrange freight and logistics
- Provide competitive pricing with reliable delivery
