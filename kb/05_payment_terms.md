# IndoGulf Trading Pvt. Ltd. — Payment Terms

## Accepted Payment Modes

### 1. Telegraphic Transfer (TT / Wire Transfer)
- Most common mode for our transactions
- **New buyers:** 100% advance before production
- **Repeat buyers (3+ orders):** 50% advance + 50% before shipment
- **Established buyers (1 year+):** 30% advance + 70% against BL copy

### 2. Letter of Credit (LC)
- Accepted from reputed banks
- Must be an **irrevocable, confirmed LC** from a scheduled bank
- LC to be opened in favour of: IndoGulf Trading Pvt. Ltd.
- LC validity: Minimum 60 days from date of issue
- LC charges on buyer's account
- Preferred for orders above $25,000

### 3. Documents Against Payment (DP)
- Available for repeat buyers with good track record
- Through our bank: HDFC Bank, Hyderabad

### 4. Escrow / Trade Finance Platforms
- Open to using verified B2B trade platforms (Treyd, Drip Capital, etc.)
- Contact us to discuss

## Bank Details (for TT payments)
- **Beneficiary:** IndoGulf Trading Pvt. Ltd.
- **Bank:** HDFC Bank Ltd.
- **Branch:** Secunderabad, Hyderabad
- **Account Type:** Current Account (INR / EEFC for USD)
- **SWIFT Code:** HDFCINBB
- Full account details shared on Pro Forma Invoice only

## Process Flow

```
Inquiry → Pro Forma Invoice (PI) sent → 
Buyer confirms PI → Advance payment → 
Production begins → Pre-shipment docs sent → 
Balance payment → Shipment released → BL handed over
```

## Important Notes
- **No goods dispatched without payment confirmation**
- PI is valid for **7 days** — prices may change after
- All bank charges outside India on buyer's account
- USD preferred; INR accepted for domestic buyers
- Crypto / UPI for international: Not accepted
