# IndoGulf Trading Pvt. Ltd. — Logistics & Export

## Export Ports (India)
- **Nhava Sheva (JNPT), Mumbai** — Primary port for most commodities
- **Mundra Port, Gujarat** — For groundnuts, sesame
- **Krishnapatnam Port, AP** — For chilli, turmeric (closer to source)
- **Chennai Port** — For cashew kernels

## Incoterms We Offer
| Term | Meaning | Who Arranges Freight |
|---|---|---|
| **EXW** | Ex Works — buyer picks from our warehouse | Buyer |
| **FOB** | Free On Board — we load on ship at origin port | Buyer |
| **CIF** | Cost Insurance Freight — we deliver to destination port | We do |
| **CFR** | Cost & Freight — like CIF without insurance | We do |

> Most buyers prefer **FOB** — we handle everything up to ship, buyer arranges from there.

## Container Types
- **FCL (Full Container Load):** 20ft (~18–20 MT) or 40ft (~24–26 MT)
- **LCL (Less than Container Load):** Available for smaller quantities, slightly higher freight cost
- **Reefer (Refrigerated):** Not required for our commodities (all dry goods)

## Customs & Compliance
- We handle Indian export customs clearance
- HS Codes provided with every shipment
- All products comply with destination country import regulations
- Fumigation done as per ISPM-15 norms

## Important Notes

- Force majeure situations (port strikes, weather) may cause delays
