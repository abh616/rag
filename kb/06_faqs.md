# IndoGulf Trading Pvt. Ltd. — FAQs & Common Questions

## About the Company

**Q: Are you a manufacturer or a trader?**
A: We are a trading company. We source directly from verified farmers and processors across India. We do not manufacture ourselves, but we work with the same suppliers consistently, so quality is controlled and reliable.

**Q: How long have you been in business?**
A: Since 2011 — over 13 years in commodity trading and export.

**Q: Are you APEDA registered?**
A: Yes. We are APEDA registered, FSSAI licensed, and ISO 9001:2015 certified.

## About Products

**Q: Can I get a sample before placing an order?**
A: Yes. We provide samples for most products. Sample weight: 100g–500g depending on product. Sample is free; courier charges are on buyer's account. Sample dispatch within 3–5 working days.

**Q: Can you do custom packing / private label?**
A: Yes. For orders above MOQ, we can do custom packing (bag size, design, label) with 10–15 extra working days.

**Q: Do you have organic certified products?**
A: Yes, for select products like sesame seeds and turmeric. Organic certification (NPOP / NOP) available. Please specify when inquiring — pricing is slightly higher.

**Q: What is the shelf life of your products?**
A: Typically 12–18 months for spices and dry commodities when stored in cool, dry conditions. Specific shelf life mentioned on the product COA.

**Q: Can you source a product not in your catalogue?**
A: Possibly. Share your requirement — we will check sourcing feasibility within 48 hours.

## About Pricing & Orders

**Q: Your MOQ is too high. Can you do smaller quantities?**
A: For first-time buyers, we can sometimes accommodate 50% of standard MOQ on select products. This depends on availability. Please ask specifically.

**Q: The price is higher than other suppliers. Can you negotiate?**
A: Our pricing reflects consistent quality, proper documentation, and reliable delivery. For volume orders, we are open to discussion. Please share the quantity and destination — we will see what we can do.

**Q: How quickly can I get a quote?**
A: For standard products, quotes are shared within a few hours. Custom or non-catalogue products may take up to 24 hours.

**Q: Do you give credit terms?**
A: Credit is available only for buyers with 3+ completed orders and a good payment track record. New buyers require advance payment.

## About Logistics & Delivery

**Q: Do you export to [specific country]?**
A: We export to most countries in the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Africa. Share your country — we will confirm and provide estimated freight and timeline.

**Q: Can you ship by air freight?**
A: Yes, for urgent small quantities or samples. Air freight cost is significantly higher. We will include it in the quote if requested.

**Q: Who handles customs at the destination?**
A: Destination customs clearance is the buyer's responsibility. We provide all necessary export documents (BL, CoO, phytosanitary certificate, etc.) to support smooth import clearance.

**Q: What if there is a quality issue after delivery?**
A: We provide pre-shipment quality certificates from accredited labs. If a dispute arises, we review the buyer's lab report and our pre-shipment report. We follow standard trade arbitration norms.

## Getting Started

**Q: How do I place my first order?**
A:
1. Tell us the product, grade, quantity, and destination port
2. We send you a Pro Forma Invoice (PI)
3. You confirm and make advance payment
4. We begin production and packing
5. We share pre-shipment documents
6. Balance payment if applicable
7. Goods shipped — BL shared
